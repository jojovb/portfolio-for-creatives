Get (Motiv)ated!


Productivity and motivational executive functioning skills are significant challenges for those who are neurodivergent or struggle with mental illness.

My objective was to create a new and easy-to-use task management system that can help neurodivergent young adults stay productive and organized throughout their daily lives.

Mentored by Gae Wakabayashi and Mark Stokoe.
