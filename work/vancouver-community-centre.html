Connecting with Community.


Vancouver’s community centres offer a wide range of activities and spaces; from fitness classes and art workshops to swimming pools and skating rinks.

As there seem to be fewer ‘third spaces’ in urban landscapes and more young people find it difficult to meet others in their local community, how might we make community services within the city of Vancouver more appealing and accessible?

Please note: This is a fictitious project, and is not official work.
