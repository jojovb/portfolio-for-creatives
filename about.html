  • About Me

    My name is Jordan, and I’m a visual designer and creative based in Vancouver, Canada. I am currently finishing my fourth year at Capilano University’s IDEA School of Design.

    I have many areas of interest that I feel deeply connected to, particularly within the world of digital media and entertainment. In other words, I’m a big nerd about certain kinds of music, films, TV shows, video games, and even online comedy (needless to say, I grew up watching a lot of Youtube).

    But aside from that, I consider myself a big thinker and feeler. I find a lot of value in continuing to explore, learn, and grow as a creative person, striving to genuinely connect with others however I can. Ultimately, I hope to continue to create work that resonates, inspires, and leaves a lasting impact.
